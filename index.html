FAQ

What is this benchmark for?+
We want everyone — especially the large AI and quantum labs — to use it to evaluate their agents. An agent that scores well here is demonstrably good at scientific discovery: it can take an open quantum problem and build a solution that beats a strong reference. Performance on Quantum's Last Exam is a direct signal of research capability, not memorized knowledge.
How is it different from other benchmarks?+
It is an evolving benchmark, created automatically. Problems are mined from the live literature, formalized, and auto-scored with no human effort required to build each environment. Because the pipeline generates and hardens its own tasks, it raises difficulty as models improve — it does not saturate the way a fixed, hand-written question set does.
How are contributors compensated?+
Every reviewer is acknowledged by name in the publication. Contribute five or more accepted tasks and your name is added to the author list.
Who can contribute?+
Any working quantum researcher — faculty, postdocs, PhD students, and industry scientists. You review problems in your own area of expertise.
What does reviewing involve?+
Pick two tasks in your field on the annotation platform and judge whether each is valid, well-posed, novel, and worth solving. A completed example is provided so the task is clear before you start.
